About

A little about me

Probal Bose is a principal site reliability engineer and a PhD researcher in computer science and financial mathematics at University College Dublin. He has spent most of his working life in infrastructure and software engineering, first building wireless networks and later running the platforms that make machine learning decisions inside payment systems, where a few milliseconds matter.

His research studies how financial markets behave at the level of individual orders, which means reading papers where a single page can use σ for volatility, σ-algebra for information and Σ for both a sum and a covariance matrix. Reading the Greek is his first book, the guide he wished he'd had on his desk. He lives in Dublin, Ireland.
