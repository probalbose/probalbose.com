One letter, four fields

The same symbol, 4 different jobs

A mathematician, a physicist, a statistician and a trader can all write σ and mean 4 different things. The book follows each letter through every field, so you know which meaning is in play.

Add it all up

Capital Σ is the summation sign, a compact instruction to add up a whole list of terms.

Hot things glow

In the Stefan–Boltzmann law, σ sets how much heat each square metre of a hot surface radiates.

How spread out

Small σ is the standard deviation, how far values typically stray from the average.

How wildly prices swing

The same σ measures how wildly a price swings, probably the most used number in risk management.
